# 🗡️ Parry Game (Name still in the works)

A **2D shooter survival game** built in **Unity (C#)**.  
You play as a **Samurai**, condemned to execution. With nothing but your blade and your resolve, your only goal is to **survive waves of incoming bullets**—deflecting them with precise timing and embracing the way of the sword.  

---

## 🎮 Gameplay

- **Stationary Samurai:** You cannot run, only your sword stands between you and death.  
- **Bullet Hell Survival:** Enemies fire projectiles in patterns that grow more intense over time.  
- **Parry & Deflect:** Time your parries perfectly to send bullets back.  
- **Endless Challenge:** The longer you survive, the more relentless your enemies become.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Engine:** Unity 6  
- **Language:** C#  
- **Platform:** PC (Windows, Mac)  
- **Graphics:** 2D pixel sprites
